# 🎮 Cave Miner V3

A fun browser-based mining game where you explore the Forgotten Mines, mine ores, fight monsters, and upgrade your equipment!

## 🎮 Play Now

**[Play the game here!](https://dthomasishere.github.io/cave-miner-v3/)**

## 🕹️ Controls

### Desktop
- **WASD** or **Arrow Keys** - Move around
- **Space** - Mine (when near ore)
- **I** - Open Inventory
- **B** - Open Shop

### Mobile
- Use the **D-Pad** buttons to move
- **⛏️ Mine** - Tap to mine
- **🎒** - Open inventory
- **🏪** - Open shop

## 🎯 Gameplay

- **Mine Ores**: Get close to ore rocks and press Space/tap Mine button to extract them
- **Collect Loot**: Gather Coal ⬛, Iron ⬜, Crystal 💎, and Gold 🟨
- **Sell Ores**: Open your inventory and click "Sell All Ores" to earn money
- **Upgrade Equipment**: Use the shop to upgrade your pickaxe (mines faster) and backpack (hold more items)
- **Avoid Monsters**: Watch out for enemies patrolling the mines - they deal damage when close!
- **Rare Encounters**: Beware of mysterious entities that may appear during your adventure

## 👹 Enemies

- **Rock Crawler** - Brown, slow but tanky
- **Cave Bat** - Purple, fast and agile
- **Stone Goblin** - Green, strong and tough
- **Magma Bug** - Red, the most dangerous regular enemy
- **Rare Entities** - Drex, Kane, and the mysterious G.O.D.
